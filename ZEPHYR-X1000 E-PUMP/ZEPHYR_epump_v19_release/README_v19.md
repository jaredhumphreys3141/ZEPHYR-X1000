# ZEPHYR-X1000 e-pump v19 release (HTP + IPA)

v19 = v18 casing + v17 rotor, with three changes:
1. Body-cartridge radial location: 2x Ø3 m6 dowels at r 28, 110° (round hole) and 290° (radial slot in the body).
   Ream the body holes in the shroud-bore setup and the cartridge holes in the bearing-bore setup.
2. Floating ring seal replaces the PCTFE throttle bushing (same Ø26 x 4 pocket, z -6.3..-2.3):
   - ring: unfilled PCTFE, OD Ø16.0, bore Ø10.050 cold (select-fit 0.050-0.060 diametral to the measured shaft at 20 °C),
     body 1.8, nose r 5.50-5.90 x 0.2
   - carrier: 316L, press-fit Ø26 n6, bore Ø16.2 (float ±0.1), Ø10.6 hole, floor turned + lapped (flatness ≤2 µm, Ra ≤0.2);
     OD, bore and floor turned in one setup. No stakes: the impeller fins capture the ring (1.5 axial play).
3. Ports: bleed (to tank) Ø1.8 drilling kept inside the 2.0 bleed groove between ring carrier and lip seal, M5 on a Ø8 boss under the flange;
   weep (overboard) Ø2.0 from the weep cavity, M5 x 6 on a Ø8 boss at r 13-19 (v18 M5 cut into the seal bore and bearing seat).

4. Inducers: R0.4 blade-root fillets on both (inducer_HTP_15mm_v19.step, inducer_IPA_12mm_v19.step), generated by
   inducer_cad_fillet.py (circular thickness flare, verified to 0.001 mm) and post_v17.py; the LE root bulge is trimmed flush
   with the nut seat. R0.4 chosen from root_strength.py: Kt 1.30 (HTP) / 1.21 (IPA), fatigue n >= 5, blockage +1.2% / +1.7%.

Files: pump_assembly_{HTP,IPA}_v19.step (all parts; bearings, spacer, seal, circlip, wave spring and locknut as envelopes),
pump_body_{HTP,IPA}_v19.step, cartridge_v19.step (common), frs_carrier_316L_v19.step, frs_ring_PCTFE_v19.step,
pump_v19_sections.png, check_v19_*.json (interference/clearance), perf_v19.json, build_v19.py, perf_v19.py, xsection_v19.py.
Rotor parts, end cap and inducers are unchanged from rotor_v17 / casing_v18.

Known modelling conventions: shaft/nut overlap = M4 thread (nut modelled at tap drill); IPA impeller/inducer 0.01 mm3 =
coincident seat face. Circlip envelope corrected to sit in its groove.
